## Before you start

- You need the owner role.
- Your organization must not have been rejected or suspended by an operator.

:::steps
### Open the invite window
In **Settings › Members & invites**, choose **Invite**.

### Enter an address and role
Enter the **Email** and pick a **Role**. A **Member** can only view; an **Admin** changes agents, tokens, and policies.

### Send it
Choose **Invite**. It shows up under **Pending invites**.
:::

## What the invitee does

1. Open the link in the invite email. Check that the invite was sent to their own address, then continue.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the login email that follows on the same page, or open its link in the same browser.

Invite emails don't include your organization's name. Let the person know the invite is coming.

## Good to know

- Invites expire after 7 days.
- Choose **Cancel invite** under **Pending invites**, and the link you already sent stops working.
- The number of people in an organization (pending invites included) and the invites you can send per day are capped. The screen tells you when you hit a cap.
