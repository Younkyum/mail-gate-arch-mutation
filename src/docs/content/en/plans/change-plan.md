## Before you start

- You need the owner or admin role. Members see that billing is handled by owners and admins.
- Your organization must not have been rejected or suspended by an operator.

:::note
Payments aren't open during the beta. Organizations that sign up from a waitlist invitation start on Free, and only organizations an operator invited use the free Beta plan.
:::

:::steps
### Open the Plan screen
Go to **Settings › Plan & usage**. You'll see your current plan and each item's usage and limit.

### Pick a new plan
Choose a plan under **New plan** and choose **Upgrade**.

### Pay
You're taken to the payment screen, where you see the price. When you're done, you come back to the console, and the change shows up once it's processed.
:::

![The Plan screen in Settings](shot:settings-plan)

## If Upgrade is locked

The ⓘ next to the button shows why. Common reasons:

- **Payments aren't open during the beta.** The Beta plan is free, and its limits are in the Beta column of [Plans and limits](/plans/plans-and-limits).
- An operator rejected or suspended your organization, or it signed up earlier and is still under operator review.
- Billing isn't ready yet. To change plans, [contact support](/help/contact).

## Manage billing

After you subscribe, choose **Manage billing** to open the billing management page. If a payment fails, the Plan screen shows a warning, and if the payment method isn't fixed by the date shown, the plan changes to Free.

## Payment, tax, and refunds

Paddle processes payments, tax and refunds as our Merchant of Record. Prices are in US dollars (USD), and tax may be added depending on where you are. Refunds follow the [refund policy](https://atmark.ai/refund).
