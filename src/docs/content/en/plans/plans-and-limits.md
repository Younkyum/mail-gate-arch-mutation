## Who each plan is for

| Plan | Who |
|---|---|
| Free | Every organization that signed up from a waitlist invitation. You start on Free as soon as you sign up, with no operator review to wait for. A paid organization also moves to Free if a failed payment isn't fixed before the grace period ends. |
| Beta | Organizations an operator invited and granted directly. Free during the beta, and it can't be bought or reached by signing up. |
| Pro · Team · Scale | Billed monthly. Start from the console. Payments aren't open during the beta. |

## Good to know

- Sign-up is by invitation from the [waitlist](https://atmark.ai/waitlist), valid for 14 days. Organizations that sign up from an invitation are active right away, so they can send without operator review. If an operator rejects or suspends an organization, sending closes. See [Plans and operator actions](/get-started/beta-and-review).
- The daily recipient limit ramps up to these values over about two weeks after the first send.
- Your plan's agent limit doesn't count disabled agents (ones operators turned off). Stopped agents do count. See [How agents are counted](/agents/create#counting).
- See your current usage under **Settings › Plan & usage** in the console.

## If you're over a limit

If you downgraded, or an operator adjusted your limits, and you now have more than the plan allows, a banner at the top of every screen says you're over your plan's limits. Existing agents, keys, and tokens keep working. You just can't create new ones until you're back under the limit.
