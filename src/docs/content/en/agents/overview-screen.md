Select a row in the agent list to open that agent. The sidebar switches to the agent's menu. Choose **← Agents** at the top to return to the list.

![An agent's overview screen](shot:agent-overview)

| Menu | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Overview | Sent, received, blocked, and held counts for the last 7 days; email, token, and identity summaries; recent activity. **Emergency stop** is at the bottom. |
| Connect | The setup message for your agent and the connection details (MCP URL, auth header). See [Choose how to connect your agent](/connect/overview). |
| Email | Outbound and inbound modes, allowlists and blocklists, the per-minute limit, and trust marks. See [Choose who your agent can email](/email/sending). |
| Webhooks | Your webhooks: add, rotate the secret, delete, plus recent deliveries and redelivery. A secret is shown only once, right after you add or rotate. See [Webhooks](/api/webhooks). |
| Logs | This agent's outbound, inbound, console, and scheduled records. See [Read the logs](/team/logs). |
| Identity | The identity card, the publish switch, and links to public documents. See [Identity card](/identity/identity-card). |
| Tokens | The token list, new tokens, rotation, and revocation. See [Manage tokens](/connect/tokens). |

## The switcher at the top

The top bar shows `organization / agent`. Choose the arrows next to the agent's name to jump to another agent.

## What changes by role

Members can see every screen but can't change anything. A **Read-only** notice appears at the top.
